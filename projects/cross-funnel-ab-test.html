Portfolio / Experiment readout

US Meta Cross-Funnel A/B Test

Does adding Mid-Funnel Engager Retargeting on top of business-as-usual Lower-Funnel Prospecting improve conversion efficiency, without hurting new-buyer quality? A standardized readout, from hypothesis through to a launch decision.

  • Market: US
  • Design: 50/50 user split
  • Hypothesis · Validity · Effect · Attribution · Heterogeneity · Decision

About the data: every number on this page comes from a synthetic training workbook (Cross_Funnel_AB_Test_Synthetic_Raw_Data.xlsx). It is not real company experiment data and doesn’t represent a real business decision. The workbook’s two datasets, Campaign_Daily_Raw and User_AB_Simulation, were built independently at different levels of granularity, so they can’t be combined for statistical inference.

01Hypothesis

Control / Cell ABAU Lower-Funnel (LF) Prospecting, with no added Mid-Funnel (MF) Retargeting
Treatment / Cell BBAU LF Prospecting + MF Engager Retargeting
SplitDesigned as a user-level 50/50 random split, observed daily at campaign level
Primary metricsGMV and GMV/Cost. Secondary: CTR, CPC, and CVR (BI/Clicks)
Quality guardrailDNC Share = DNC / All users, to check for a trade-off in new-buyer value
HypothesisH1: Treatment GMV/Cost is higher than Control. H0: there is no difference between the groups.

Decision prerequisite: both groups must use comparable attribution windows, product scope, budgets, and event definitions, and the user-level split should be verified from platform logs.

02Validity

A 50/50 user split is the intended randomization, but campaign summary tables alone can’t prove that assignment was mutually exclusive, that users were balanced, or that no one was exposed to both groups. The original experiment had no A/A test, and this training version doesn’t include assignment logs, user identity matching, or pre-experiment covariates.

Verdict: the data supports a descriptive comparison, but isn’t yet enough to confirm that the campaign-level GMV lift is statistically significant or causal.

04Attribution

GMV here is attributed transaction value under a set measurement definition. “Treatment has higher attributed efficiency” isn’t the same as “MF exposure created the same amount of incremental value on its own.” The experiment compares the overall effect of adding MF Retargeting as a strategy package, not the standalone contribution of a single MF touchpoint.

QuestionCurrent evidence and what’s needed
Click / view attributionThe training table has no click/view split. The window needs to be fixed and documented, for example 1-day click / 0-day view.
Conversion lagRead results after a mature conversion window, so Treatment and Control are backfilled to the same point
MF–LF overlapThe source case stresses cross-funnel reach overlap, but the training table has no user-level exposure paths
IncrementalityRequires valid randomization and independent measurement. Attributed GMV can’t be called causal GMV.
DNC qualityCell B’s absolute DNC rose while DNC Share fell, so volume and mix need to be read separately

  • Scope: these core segments exclude PNA, which stays in the training data to practice keeping scope consistent. Segment results are exploratory: with multiple comparisons and limited sample sizes, no single segment difference can be called significant yet.

06Decision

Conditional iterate

Keep testing under clear conditions. This training data alone doesn’t justify a full launch.

Summary: the synthetic data suggests BAU + MF Retargeting delivers higher attributed efficiency, but with a weaker new-buyer mix and diverging segment results. Validate the experiment and the attribution definitions first, then consider a phased rollout.
